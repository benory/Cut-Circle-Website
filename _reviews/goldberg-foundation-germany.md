<p>Simply the most perfect recording I have been waiting for for decades! Perfectly intelligible rendering to the texts where you can not only realize the beauty of the poetry but also the strong emotions. The radical a cappella approach is very convincing as the counterpoint and the beauty of the lines comes out impressively. Last but not least, wonderful sound recording!</p>
