As a nonprofit organization with strong ties to Stanford University, Cut Circle brings the distant culture of Renaissance feasting to life through the Community Feasting Project. To fulfill this mission we collaborate with top culinary talent in the Bay Area community.

Our events are a cross between pop-up dinners, concerts, and interactive educational experiences: guests eat, listen, and join us in singing songs from the great age of feasting. Audiences leave wined, dined, and wiser about the history of food and music.
