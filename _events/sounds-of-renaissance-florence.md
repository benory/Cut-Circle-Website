<p>Inside, outside, and in the streets—hear the bellowing of sacred texts and the howling of carnival songs, the devotion of the faithful and the revelry of the drunk.</p>
<p>Cut Circle joins Stanford students from Music 159J (“Performance as Analysis”) to sing from—and invent multi-voice music based on—a perfectly preserved fifteenth-century Florentine chant manuscript. The book forms part of the sumptuous <a href="https://exhibits.stanford.edu/burke_mss">Burke Collection of Early Italian Miniatures</a>, which will be on view during the performance as part of the exhibition The Illuminated Page: Decorated Medieval Choirbooks from the Burke Collection.</p>
