<p>To love another—would humiliate my heart,” sings the devoted lover in Johannes Ockeghem’s famous “D’ung aultre amer,” evoking the power of love to captivate, nourish, and, on occasion, destroy. Love can be earthly and sensual; it can also be spiritual and divine, as when a Biblical figure is cast as the object of desire.</p>
<p>“To Love Another…” will feature the music of both romantic and spiritual intensity by Du Fay, Ockeghem, Josquin, and their contemporaries.</p>
