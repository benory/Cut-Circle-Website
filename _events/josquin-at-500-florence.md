<p>Cut Circle celebrates the quincentenary of Josquin des Prez with vivid, soulful performances of motets and chansons by the Prince of Musicians.</p>







<p>27 August (Friday, <a href="https://www.polifonico.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/polifonico_convegno-scaled.jpg">Arezzo</a>—anniversary of Josquin’s death)<br>12:30pm: Short concert preview (Church of San Michele)<br>3–6pm: Josquin conference, featuring an appearance by Cut Circle (Sala Vasariana)<br>9pm: performance of Josquin’s <em>Pater noster</em>, with the Italian ensembles Odhecaton and De Labyrintho (Church of Santa Maria della Pieve)</p>



<p>28 August (Saturday, <a href="https://www.polifonico.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/polifonico_programma.pdf">Arezzo</a>) <br>6pm: concert (Church of San Michele)</p>



<p>29 August (Sunday, <a href="https://hommearme.it/en/events/floremus-evening-concert-ensemble-cut-circle-josquin-500/">Florence</a>)<br>9:15pm: concert (Michelozzo Library, San Marco Museum)</p>
