<p>Calling all sight-readers for an evening of vocal polyphony, led by Jesse Rodin and hosted by Katherine McKee of St. Bede’s Episcopal Church. The event is free. RSVP <a href="http://evite.me/7a6WrdCggC">here</a> (use the “poll” to enter your voice part).</p>
<p><strong><em>Please print the music yourself or bring your preferred device. If this proves a hardship, let us know and we’ll be happy to help.</em></strong></p>
<p>REPERTOIRE<br>
1. Byrd, <em>Ave verum corpus</em>; <em>Sing Joyfully Unto God</em> (hard copies provided)<br>
2. Josquin, <em><a href="{{ '/assets/documents/Jos2402-Gaude-virgo-Elders-NJE.pdf' | relative_url }}"><em>Gaude virgo mater Christi</em></a></em><br>
3. Anonymous, <em><a href="{{ '/assets/documents/Missa-Caput-anon-English-ficta-edited.pdf' | relative_url }}"><em>Missa Caput</em></a></em><br>
4. Marenzio, from <em><a href="http://conquest.imslp.info/files/imglnks/usimg/7/76/IMSLP288755-PMLP171843-00-score-book.pdf">Madrigali à 4 voci, libro primo</a></em> (1584):<br>
     “Zefiro torna” (score p. 51, two <em>partes</em>; <a href="http://www1.cpdl.org/wiki/index.php/Zefiro_torna_e_il_bel_tempo_rimena">text</a>)<br>
     “Non vidi mai dopo notturna pioggia” (score p. 1; <a href="http://www1.cpdl.org/wiki/index.php/Non_vidi_mai_dopo_notturna_pioggia_(Luca_Marenzio)">text</a>)<br>
5. Salamone Rossi, <a href="{{ '/assets/documents/Rossi-Haleluyah-haleli-nafshi.pdf' | relative_url }}"><em>Haleluyah, haleli nafshi</em></a></p>
