<p>Lecture by Jesse Rodin, as part of the course <em>The Genius of Leonardo da Vinci: A 500th Anniversary Celebration</em>. Professor Rodin will cover Leonardo’s views on and inventions in the realm of music, as well as a series of exciting “technological” innovations in musical composition that Leonardo encountered during his career. </p>
