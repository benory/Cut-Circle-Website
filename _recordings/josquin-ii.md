**Released February 2026.** Featuring the *Missa L’ami Baudichon* and the Milanese motet cycles *Vultum tuum deprecabuntur* and *Qui velatus facie fuisti*.

This second volume in Cut Circle’s complete Josquin cycle focuses on sacred music from before 1490. Josquin’s compositional prowess, even during what seems to have been his first working decade as a composer, is simply astonishing.
