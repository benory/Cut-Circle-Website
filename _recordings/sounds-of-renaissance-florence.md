Inside, outside, and in the streets—hear the bellowing of sacred texts and the howling of carnival songs, the devotion of the faithful and the revelry of the drunk.

A stunning chant book in Stanford’s Special Collections anchors a short film connecting Cut Circle’s flexible vocal technique to the soundscape of fifteenth-century Italy.
