Cut Circle offers fresh interpretations of modern-day classics like *Miserere mei deus*, *Scaramella*, and *Ave Maria…virgo serena* alongside lesser-known works that deserve to be appreciated on the same footing.

The album introduces a new sound for Josquin that showcases his brilliance, versatility, and power.
