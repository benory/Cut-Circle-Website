- A landmark recording of Du Fay’s four late masses: *Se la face ay pale*, *L’homme armé*, *Ecce ancilla domini*, and *Ave regina celorum*.
- Close miking recreates the intimate experience of standing shoulder-to-shoulder with Renaissance singers.
- Distinguished by lively tempi, dynamic phrasing, and rhythmic energy.
- A generous two-CD set with a lavish booklet, including color images and liner notes in four languages.
