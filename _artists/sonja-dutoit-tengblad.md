Described as “radiant” by *Opera Magazine*, recent solo highlights for soprano **Sonja DuToit Tengblad** include Shostakovich’s *Symphony No. 14* with A Far Cry, Mahler’s *Second Symphony* with the Boston Philharmonic, Barber’s *Knoxville: Summer of 1915* with the Boston Landmarks Orchestra, Monteverdi’s *L’incoronazione di Poppea* (Drusilla, Fortuna) with Boston Baroque, Puccini’s *Suor Angelica* with the Boston Symphony Orchestra and Lorelei Ensemble, Bach’s *St. John Passion* with the [Handel and Haydn Society](https://handelandhaydn.org/), Knussen’s *Symphony No. 2* with the [Boston Modern Orchestra Project](https://www.bmop.org/), and her Carnegie Hall and Lincoln Center debuts with the New York City Chamber Orchestra. She was awarded second place in the 2014 American Prize competition’s art song and oratorio division.

A champion of modern music, Tengblad curated the award-winning touring program [*Modern Dickinson*](http://www.moderndickinson.com/) and launched the recital project BeatSong for soprano and percussion. She sings with the Grammy-winning ensemble Conspirare on tours of *Considering Matthew Shepard* and is a core member of Lorelei Ensemble. She founded Beyond Artists in 2019 and with every performance donates to Braver Angels in addition to the Eden Reforestation Project or Singers of This Age.
